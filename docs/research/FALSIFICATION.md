# FALSIFICATION.md
## AETHER — Conditions of Refutation

---

### 0. Principle

Every hypothesis must state,
**in advance**,
what observable outcome would invalidate it.

Thresholds are not defined here.
Thresholds will be determined after baseline characterization (BASELINE.md).

---

### 1. P-01 — Behavioral Momentum

**Hypothesis:**
Temporal continuity increases action autocorrelation.

**Falsification Condition:**
After baseline characterization and controlled experiments,
if the experimental group shows **no measurable increase**
in action autocorrelation compared to the control group,
the hypothesis is rejected.

*Measurable* is defined in BASELINE.md after data collection.

---

### 2. P-02 — Self Preservation

**Hypothesis:**
Self-model causes proactive rest and avoidance of costly actions.

**Falsification Condition:**
If the agent never rests before energy falls below a critical threshold
(defined during baseline),
or if fatigue does not measurably reduce exploration,
the hypothesis is rejected.

---

### 3. P-03 — Counterfactual Reasoning

**Hypothesis:**
Simulation changes actual action selection.

**Falsification Condition:**
If simulation does not measurably alter action selection
compared to a control without simulation,
the hypothesis is rejected.

---

### 4. P-04 — Internal Conflict

**Hypothesis:**
Competing internal drives produce non-deterministic decision periods.

**Falsification Condition:**
If decisions are always resolved instantly,
or if they oscillate indefinitely without resolution,
the hypothesis is rejected.

---

### 5. P-05 — Identity

**Hypothesis:**
Behavior becomes increasingly predictable from history.

**Falsification Condition:**
If history does not provide measurable predictive power
beyond current state and reward,
the hypothesis is rejected.

---

### 6. P-06 — Intrinsic Projects

**Hypothesis:**
Persistent behavioral trajectories emerge
without explicit goal programming.

**Falsification Conditions (Requires ALL to be true for rejection):**
- No trajectory persists beyond a measurable duration
  (duration defined during baseline).
- The agent never measurably sacrifices reward for a goal.
- Failure causes immediate abandonment.
- Goals do not mutate or generate new goals.

---

### 7. P-07 — Creativity

**Hypothesis:**
Novel structures appear that are not explained by exploration noise.

**Falsification Condition:**
If the novelty of output is strictly proportional to
the exploration noise injected into the generator,
the hypothesis is rejected.

---

### 8. General Falsification Rule

If an experiment yields positive results
**but the control experiment yields identical results**,
the hypothesis is rejected immediately.

No exceptions.

---

### 9. Post-Hoc Adjustment Policy

No threshold may be adjusted after the experiment is run.

All thresholds must be declared in BASELINE.md
**before** the experiment begins.
